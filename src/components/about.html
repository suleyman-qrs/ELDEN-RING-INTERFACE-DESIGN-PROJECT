Hidetaka Miyazaki —

Hidetaka Miyazaki (宮崎 英高) is a Japanese video game director and writer,
best known for his work on the Souls series, Bloodborne, Sekiro,
and Elden Ring.

Born in 1974, Miyazaki developed an interest in video games at a
young age, but initially pursued a career in business. He joined
FromSoftware in 2004 and quickly rose through the ranks to become
a director.

Miyazaki's games are known for their challenging gameplay, intricate
world-building, and deep lore. He has been praised for his ability to
create immersive and atmospheric game worlds that captivate players
and encourage exploration.

In addition to his work in game development, Miyazaki has also
expressed interest in other forms of storytelling, including film and
literature. He has cited authors such as J.R.R. Tolkien and H.P. Lovecraft as influences on his work.

Miyazaki continues to be a highly influential figure in the gaming industry, and his work has garnered a dedicated fanbase around the world
