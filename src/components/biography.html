This is where you awaken, stepping into the fog as one of the Tarnished—a warrior long exiled from the light of the Erdtree. With the guidance of grace, you are tasked with venturing across treacherous landscapes to defeat the corrupt demigods, collect their Great Runes, and reassemble the ring. Rather than following a rigid path, you write your own story as you navigate sprawling castles, plagued swamps, and ancient dungeons, piecing together the world's cryptic lore through environmental storytelling and chance encounters.
