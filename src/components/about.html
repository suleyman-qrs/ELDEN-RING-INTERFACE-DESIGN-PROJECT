Hidetaka miyazaki —

Hidetaka Miyazaki (宮崎 英高) is a Japanese video game director, designer, writer, and president of FromSoftware. He joined the company in 2004 and was a designer for the Armored Core series before receiving wider recognition for creating the Dark Souls series. Miyazaki was promoted to company president in 2014.


His games invoke high difficulty and narratives presented through flavor text and environmental cues rather than dialogue. Regarded as an auteur, Miyazaki's works are cited among the greatest in the medium, leading to the creation of the Soulslike subgenre.
