ELDEN RING

This is a non-profit fan made narrative web experience. Our goal is to immerse users into an interactive teaser for Elden Ring, a Fromsoftware game released in 2022.
The art and the music doesn’t belongs to us but are taken from the game and official art books as well as two illustrations made by Caio Santos (Malenia) and Olga Shvetskaya (Godrick) both active on ArtStation.
