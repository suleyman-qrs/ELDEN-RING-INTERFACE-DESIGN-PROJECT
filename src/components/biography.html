Long ago, you were exiled from the Lands Between.

The grace of gold forsook your kind, and you wandered far beyond its light—dead, yet living still.

Ages passed in the dark, and your name, your purpose, faded from memory.

Now a small grace flickers anew, and calls to you alone.

It bids you return to the land that once cast you out.

And so you find yourself in the Roundtable Hold—a quiet refuge where the last of the Tarnished gather, and where your journey begins.
