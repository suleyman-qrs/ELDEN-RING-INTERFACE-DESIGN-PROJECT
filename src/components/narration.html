Elden Ring. O, Elden Ring.

Giving life its
fullest brilliance.

Its gold commanded
the very stars,

Shattered, by someone,
or something.

Godrick, the feeble.

Malenia, decayed from birth.

General Radahn,
slayer of giants.

Rykard,
the tyrannical serpent.

And Morgott,
Prince of the Omen.

Each, inheriting their own shard,
played a part in the Shattering,

a war with no end,
and no victor.

And so the Two Fingers
call upon ye, the Tarnished.

To cross the Sea of Fog,
to the Lands Between.

To seek the Elden Ring.
Seek the Elden Ring.
