You find yourself in the Roundtable Hold—a refuge outside the Lands Between, where the last of the Tarnished gather under the guidance of grace. Called back from exile by a light only you can follow, you are given purpose: seek out the shattered Great Runes, challenge the demigods who hoard them, and restore the Elden Ring. From this hall you will venture out into a broken world of towering legacy dungeons, poisoned valleys, and war-scarred plains—each return to the Hold a moment of quiet before the next descent.
